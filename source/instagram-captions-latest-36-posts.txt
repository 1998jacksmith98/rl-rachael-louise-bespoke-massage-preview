What does a “knot” feel like during a massage? 💆‍♂️

When people talk about knots, they’re usually describing areas of muscle that feel tight, tense or sensitive.

For the client, it can feel like:
💙A tender spot or “good ache” when pressure is applied
💙Small pea like bumps or little “balls” under the skin
💙A tight pulling sensation that may refer elsewhere
💙A feeling of release as the area relaxes

For the therapist, these areas can feel like:
💙Firmer bands or small areas of resistance within the muscle
💙Tissue that doesn’t glide as smoothly
💙A gradual softening as the muscle relaxes under the hands

Why is working on “knots” so satisfying? 💆‍♂️✨

For the client, it can feel rewarding because they often notice:
💙A tight area finally relaxing
💙A sense of release & lightness
💙Improved movement & less restriction
💙That satisfying “good ache” followed by relief

For the therapist, it’s satisfying because you can feel the tissue change under your hands. areas that feel tight or restricted may begin to soften & move more freely.
